Free template

Fire & EMS AI Use Annex

A ready-to-edit annex that adds fire, rescue, and EMS rules to your city or county AI policy. Six pages, free, no sign-up.

Download the PDF

What's inside.

  • Data rules. Which patient, incident, personnel, and security data can go into which kind of AI tool.
  • A four-tier risk matrix. Two questions, how much the AI does on its own and how bad an error would be, set the rules for every use. Adapted from NIST SP 1500-29.
  • Approvals and oversight by tier. Who signs off, who checks the output, and what training comes first.
  • Rules for common uses. ePCR narratives, training material, social media, accreditation, hiring, fireground tools, and AI-enabled equipment.
  • Ten vendor questions. What to ask before you buy software or equipment with AI features.
  • An AI use register. One row per approved use, ready to fill in.

Make it yours. Replace the bracketed fields, and review it with city IT, risk management, and your city attorney before you adopt it. The template is advisory and is not legal advice.
