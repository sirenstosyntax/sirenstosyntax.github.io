DrillGround

Training you can run this shift.

Live now. Free on the web, no account needed.

DrillGround is built around one test: every output should make firefighters better at the job. It supports the people who run training. It doesn't pretend to be one of them.

  • Drill plans that fit your department

    Structured, practical drill plans shaped around staffing, time, and what the shift can run. They are written to be adapted by the officer running them, and are always subordinate to your SOPs and SOGs.

  • Drills that fit the crew you have

    Teaching points, common mistakes, safety notes, and a way to run the drill when you don't have a full crew, so the new person and the veteran both get something out of it.

  • Development you can see

    Turn drills and prep into a record of growth for individuals and companies, so training divisions can see development over time and not only hours logged.

What DrillGround is not

  • Not a replacement for instructors. Qualified instruction and officer judgment stay in charge. DrillGround advises and prepares.
  • Not your SOPs or SOGs. Your department's policies govern. DrillGround works within them, never over them.
  • Not a compliance claim. I don't say "certified" or "compliant" where it hasn't been earned. Outputs are advisory and human-reviewed.

BadgeDay

For candidates trying to get hired, and for firefighters testing up.

Live now in the browser and on Google Play.

Recruit helps hire-track candidates practice the oral board and become stronger candidates. Promote helps sitting firefighters and engineers study the books on their promotional list. It is practice and prep that builds readiness, not a guaranteed hire or promotion.

  • Recruit

    For people looking to get hired. A full five-question oral board. Answer out loud and get notes on what went well and what to improve. The first complete board is free, with no card.

  • Promote

    For firefighters and engineers already on the job who are testing up. Upload your department's policies and SOPs. Practice questions come from those documents, not a generic guide.

Pricing

Recruit: first board free, then $24.99/mo, $59 for 90 days, $119 for 6 months, or $179/yr. Promote: $29/mo or $129 for 90 days.
