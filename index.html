The problem

Training time is the scarcest resource in the firehouse.

Between calls, inspections, maintenance, and everything else on the board, structured training gets squeezed. The result isn't a lack of effort — it's a lack of tools built for how fire departments actually work.

Drill planning starts from zero

Company officers rebuild drills from scratch between calls. Quality varies shift to shift and station to station, and good drills leave with the people who wrote them.

Promotional prep is scattered

Study guides, old exams, borrowed binders. There's no structured path from firefighter to engineer to officer — just whatever the last person who tested can hand you.

Progress disappears

What happens on the drill ground rarely becomes a record you can build on. Training divisions track completions, not development.

First product · Live now
DrillGround
Train. Prepare. Perform.

Training, development, and promotional prep — on one platform.

DrillGround is built around one test: every output should make firefighters better at the job. It supports the people who run training — it doesn't pretend to be one of them.

Drill plans that fit your department

Structured, practical drill plans shaped around your apparatus, staffing, and time constraints — written to be adapted by the officer running them, and always subordinate to your SOPs/SOGs.

Promotional & assessment prep

A structured path for members preparing to test — study plans, scenario practice, and honest feedback that builds readiness instead of memorization.

Development you can see

Turn drills and prep into a record of growth for individuals and companies, so training divisions can see development over time — not just hours logged.

What DrillGround is not
  • Not a replacement for instructors.Qualified instruction and officer judgment stay in charge. DrillGround advises and prepares.
  • Not your SOPs/SOGs.Your department's policies govern. DrillGround works within them, never over them.
  • Not a compliance claim.We don't say "certified" or "compliant" where we haven't earned it. Outputs are advisory and human-reviewed.

How we build

No hype. No borrowed authority.

AI is a tool in this shop, not the pitch. These are the commitments every Sirens to Syntax product is held to.

Judgment stays human

Software advises. Instructors, company officers, and department policy decide. Anything that blurs that line doesn't ship.

Built from the inside

Designed around real firehouse constraints — shift schedules, call volume, budgets, and the fact that training happens between alarms.

Evidence over hype

Features earn their place by making firefighters measurably better at the job. If it's just impressive, it's not enough.

Reviewed by people who've done the job

Outputs are advisory and human-reviewed. When we're not sure, we say so — the fire service deserves straight answers.

The founder

Built by someone who's ridden backwards.

20+ Years in emergency services

Grant Collings spent more than two decades in emergency services, rising to fire captain — planning drills, developing crews, and preparing members for promotion firsthand. Sirens to Syntax exists because the tools he needed as a company officer never did.

Now pursuing a Master's in artificial intelligence with a focus on AI security, Grant builds at the intersection of two disciplines that both demand the same thing: trust earned through competence. The fire service taught him what good training looks like. Engineering is how he's scaling it.
